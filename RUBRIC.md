# Key-Locked Door Rubric

- The main-hall enemy drops a key only after being defeated, and the player can collect it.
- The main-hall key is required to pass through both doors leading to Room 1.
- The Room 1 enemy drops a second key only after being defeated, and the player can collect it.
- The Room 1 key is required to pass through both doors leading to Room 2.
- Doors remain locked without their required key and provide clear feedback when the player attempts to open them.
- Existing enemy combat, player movement, camera controls, dungeon environment, and other existing gameplay remain functional.
