# Inventory and Item System Rubric

- The player can pick up items found in the dungeon and store them in an inventory.
- Health potions can be selected and used from the inventory to restore player health.
- Used health potions are removed from the inventory.
- The player can remove or drop items from the inventory.
- Dropped items are placed back into the dungeon and can be collected again.
- Keys can be stored, dropped, collected again, and still work with their corresponding locked doors.
- Existing combat, key progression, locked doors, player movement, camera, and health systems remain functional.
