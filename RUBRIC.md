# Enemy Combat Rubric

- Dungeon contains exactly 3 enemies: 1 in the main hall and 1 in each of the two other rooms, with none in the starting room.
- Player and enemies can fight each other, and enemies can target and damage the player with projectiles.
- Enemy projectiles are blocked by dungeon walls and cannot damage the player through walls.
- When the player dies, they restart from the starting area.
- Existing dungeon, player movement, and camera controls remain functional.
